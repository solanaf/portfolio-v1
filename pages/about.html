About me

I am a Bioengineering master's student at U.C. San Diego pursuing research in the Ung Translational Neurophysiology lab. I work on the testing, development, and deployment of quantitative EEG and artificial intelligence tools, including transformer-based seizure detection and LLM-assisted structuring of electronic medical records, to improve the efficiency and efficacy of managing epilepsy in the inpatient setting.

In addition to my thesis, I have worked with other types of biological data, including RNA-seq, DNA-seq, and ATAC-seq. My interdisciplinary studies include, but are not limited to, neuroscience, computer science, AI, and bioinformatics.

What I do

  • Neuroinformatics

    Using electrophysiology data from human EEG and non-human primate penetrative electrodes to develop clinical algorithms and understand neural mechanisms of learning, respectively.

  • Bioinformatics

    Retrieving raw RNA-seq, ATAC-seq, and DNA-seq FASTQ files, preprocessing with CLI tools, and analyzing in Python and R to identify meaningful features and generate survival models.

  • Large Language Models

    Leveraging LLMs to extract meaningful features from messy & variant EEG reports.

  • CAD & EDA

    Designed in SOLIDWORKS, I 3D-print using both SLA and FDM technologies. I also possess some experience building integrated circuits with Cadence Virtuoso.
