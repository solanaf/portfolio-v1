Rhesus Macaque Skull Segmentation

Segmented in 3D-Slicer from MRI imaging data to generate an anatomically accurate representation of the rhesus macaque skull for surgical planning and implant alignment.

Chamber Grid

Custom adapter designed in SOLIDWORKS to securely sit in the neural recording chamber during MRI imaging. These 0.5um gridholes spaced 0.25um apart mimick the arrangement on the NAN drive recording interface, intending to provide the user with an accurate guide of electrode trajectory during recording sessions.

Optimized for SLA printing to achieve precise gridhole size and placement. Extensive manual processing through microfluidic techniques. Circular ridges on the size allow for holding with external screws; circular loops allow for ease of manipulation with micro forceps.

Falcon Tube-NAN Drive Adapter

Joystick
